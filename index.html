About

A designer who thinks like a writer, facilitator and producer.

I started writing SEO articles and blog posts, then trained hundreds of learners on soft skills, then fell in love with instructional design. Today I build e-learning that respects the learner’s time: clean storyboards, warm voice-overs and interactions that mean something.

I work with L&D teams and boutique studios across the UAE and remote clients, delivering Storyline modules, Rise courses, Vyond explainers and blended programmes end-to-end.

  • Based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates
  • Languages English, Tamil, Hindi and Malayalam
  • Education BA English Literature, University of Madras (2017–2020)
