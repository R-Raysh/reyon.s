About Reyon

A designer who thinks like a writer, facilitator and producer.

I started writing SEO articles and blog posts, then trained hundreds of learners on soft skills, before discovering my passion for instructional design. I create learning experiences that respect people’s time: clear storyboards, warm voice-overs and interactions with a purpose.

I partner with L&D teams and boutique studios across the UAE and with remote clients, delivering Storyline modules, Rise courses, Vyond explainers and blended programmes from concept to launch.

  • Based in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates
  • Languages English, Tamil, Hindi and Malayalam
  • Education BA English Literature, University of Madras (2017–2020)
